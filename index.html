A full home, entirely yours

Chitova's is a four-bedroom home in the Aerodrome Suburb of Victoria Falls - ten minutes from the Falls, five minutes walk from town, and entirely yours for your stay.

Sleeps up to eight guests across four bedrooms and three bathrooms, with a fully equipped kitchen, a private pool with a rock waterfall feature, and daily breakfast prepared by our team.

And it's entirely yours - no other guests, ever. A staff member stays on site around the clock to keep the generator, water pumps, and everything else running quietly in the background.
